## What to build

**Whisky Finder** home is the first page a Customer sees — not the Catalogue.

Stand up the app in this order: app shell (top bar, nav, footer, logos) matching `docs/design/landing.html` and `docs/design/DESIGN.md`, then the **landing / home** inside that shell. House pick comes from live Supabase. Main CI deploys Vercel preview, runs Playwright e2e for shell + home, then production.

Logos: `public/brand/emblem.png` (mark) and `public/brand/lockup.jpg` (mark + WHISKY FINDER). Do not hotlink the Stitch HTML’s Google image URLs.

First-slice home includes what the mockup shows that we already agreed: chrome, House pick (уиски на месеца), club teaser as a CTA only, rails of live Whiskies if the DB has any, Finder CTA (“Открий своето идеално уиски”). First visit also shows the 18+ splash (remembered in the browser). Not in this ticket: 50ml Packs, tasting-video overlay, wishlist, awards/gifts/promotions/club **pages**, working search, working cart, a campaign/discount engine, or Shopkeeper UI to replace the House pick.

Nav “Селекция” points at Catalogue when that ticket exists; until then omit dead items rather than fake shops. Mockup labels that are out of first-ship (подаръци, промоции, награждавани, клуб pages) stay hidden or unlinked.
