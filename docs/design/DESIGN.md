## Brand & Style

This design system channels the exclusivity, atmosphere, and tactile solemnity of an elite private members' spirits club, bespoke archival distillery vaults, and rare vintage auctions. It targets discerning whisky collectors, connoisseurs, and affluent lifestyle patrons seeking rare single malts, barrel allocations, and discovery flights.

The aesthetic philosophy fuses **Tactile Luxury Minimalism** with **Editorial Heritage Elegance**:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep obsidian shadowplay, amber glow reminiscent of back-lit crystal decanters, burnished copper hardware, and charred cask charcoal textures.
- **Tone:** Authoritative, reverent, sensual, and discreet. Interfaces favor quiet confidence over decorative noise.
- **Physicality:** Subtle warm-metallic light refraction, hairline gold delineation, velvet-smooth micro-transitions, and tactile card surfaces that mimic heavy-weight uncoated cotton stationery and aged oak barrel heads.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the chromatic chemistry of distillation—fire, aged grain, charred white oak, and liquid amber.

### Primary Spectrum: Pure Whisky Amber
- **Liquid Core (`#D97706`):** The primary brand hue, used for focal interaction points, hero highlights, and active state indicators.
- **Amber Glow (`#F59E0B`):** Radiant accent for active hover states, amber glow shadows, and radar visualization nodes.
- **Deep Cask Russet (`#B45309`):** Grounding tone for subtle borders, gradient stops, and high-proof indicators.

### Accent Spectrum: Burnished Precious Metallics
- **Brushed Gold (`#E5A93C`):** High-tier classification stamps, verification crests, and pricing indicators.
- **Burnished Copper (`#C28B38`):** Editorial sub-rules, filigree strokes, and allocation lock badges.

### Neutral Foundation: Charred Oak & Obsidian
- **Void Obsidian (`#0C0D0E`):** Root canvas background. Provides total light absorption, elevating amber elements.
- **Charcoal Stave (`#141618`):** Primary card container surface and structural sidebar background.
- **Smoked Ash (`#1A1D20`):** Floating dialogs, elevated drawers, and segmented controls.
- **Muted Oak Slate (`#282A2E`):** Resting state component outlines, dividers, and disabled interactive states.
- **Aged Iron (`#3F4248`):** Secondary borders, radar chart axis grids, and inactive track indicators.
- **Silver Haze (`#9CA3AF`):** Secondary metadata, technical specifications (ABV, Cask Type, Region), and field labels.

### Typography Neutrals: Cream & Bone
- **Distillery Bone (`#F9F8F6`):** Primary display text, price points, and active typographic icons.
- **Linen Cream (`#EDE8DF`):** Body paragraphs, editorial summaries, and longform tasting narratives.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional tension between ancestral craft and contemporary technical precision:

- **Playfair Display (Display & Headlines):** Imparts the gravitas of vintage spirits labels, heritage books, and luxury packaging. It must be set with high vertical rhythm and slight negative tracking on large scales.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body, Labels, Technical Data):** Ensures razor-sharp legibility across dark backgrounds for dense bottle specifications (e.g., *“54.2% ABV · 18 Years · First-Fill Oloroso Sherry Butt”*), filter mechanics, and cart interactions.
- **Uppercase & Letterspacing:** All tier badges, category headers, and technical labels (`label-sm`, `label-md`) strictly employ uppercase casing with expanded tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to evoke formal archival stamping.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model balances generous negative space with structured editorial proportions, framing products like fine jewelry or rare art.

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views operate on a 12-column fluid grid system with `1.5rem` gutters and outer canvas margins of `3rem`, maxing out at `1440px` content container width to preserve scanability.
- **Breakpoint Transitions:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` screen margins. Product grids switch to a single column or an offset horizontal snap-scroller for tasting flights.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `2rem` screen margins. 
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `3rem` screen margins.
- **Spacing Cadence:** Vertical rhythm leans expansive. Section spacing maintains a minimum of `3rem` (`space-xl`), allowing high-contrast imagery and decanter photography breathing room without UI overcrowding.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through tonal layering, hairline metallic borders, and amber-tinted luminescent diffusion rather than opaque drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Floor Canvas):** Absolute base `#0C0D0E`. Zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Vault Panels):** Surface `#141618` bordered with a 1px hairline stroke in `#282A2E`. No shadow at rest.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Dropdowns):** Surface `#1A1D20`. Hairline border elevates to burnished copper `#C28B38` at 30% opacity. Shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 16px 0 rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Tasting Drawers, Sticky Toolbars):** Background `#141618` at 92% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a top hairline highlight stroke in `#E5A93C` at 25% opacity. Shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85), 0 0 24px 2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (`1`)** shape language to reinforce the architectural, tailored feel of premium packaging:
- **Base Components (Cards, Inputs, Buttons):** Standard radius of `0.25rem` (4px). Clean, tailored corners evoke bespoke label cutting and wooden presentation boxes.
- **Secondary Containers (Modals, Flyouts):** Corner radius of `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Badge Pills & Tasting Notes:** Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) are strictly reserved for olfactory/flavor tags and bottle size selectors to create an ergonomic visual contrast against the crisp rectangular grid.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Glowing Amber CTA:** Background gradient from `#D97706` to `#B45309`, text `#0C0D0E` (bold weight), with an ambient amber glow box-shadow (`0 4px 20px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.35)`). On hover, subtle vertical translation (-1px) and ambient glow expansion (`0 6px 28px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.5)`).
- **Secondary Cask Outline:** Transparent fill, 1px solid border `#C28B38`, text `#EDE8DF`. On hover, fills with `#1A1D20` and border changes to `#E5A93C`.
- **Ghost Action:** Pure text in `#E5A93C` with an underline micro-animation spanning outward from center on hover.

### Product & Vault Cards
- Base surface in `#141618` with a 1px border in `#282A2E`.
- Card header houses the tier indicator badge in the top right.
- High-contrast decanter photography sits on a subtle gradient vignette (radial glow centered behind bottle: `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.06)`).
- Card footer contains dual price points (Full Bottle vs Sample), instant add-to-archive interaction, and technical age/cask specifications.

### Tasting Note Badge Pills
- Rounded pill geometry (`9999px`), padding `4px 12px`.
- Background `#1A1D20`, border 1px solid `#3F4248`, typography `label-sm` in `#EDE8DF`.
- Flavor tags with thematic tinting:
  - *Peat / Smoke:* Charcoal tint `#1A1D20` with stroke `#3F4248`.
  - *Sherry / Rich Fruit:* Deep plum undertone (`#221417`) with stroke `#78350F`.
  - *Vanilla / Honey:* Warm amber undertone (`#211A11`) with stroke `#C28B38`.

### Interactive Flavor Profile Sliders & Radar Charts
- **Sliders:** 2px track height in `#282A2E`; filled active segment in `#D97706`. Thumb handle is a 12px solid circle in `#F9F8F6` enveloped by a 2px ring in `#E5A93C`.
- **Radar Charts:** Pentagonal or hexagonal axis grid constructed with 1px lines in `#282A2E`. Data polygon rendered with a `#D97706` fill at 25% opacity and a solid 1.5px `#E5A93C` boundary stroke with luminous nodes.

### Sample Bottle Selector (700ml Bottle vs 50ml Discovery Sample)
- Dual-segment toggle contained within `#141618` with an inset `2px` track.
- Active segment receives `#1A1D20` surface elevation, 1px border `#E5A93C`, and crisp cream text `#F9F8F6`.
- Inactive segment renders in muted gray `#9CA3AF` with transparent border.

### Curated Tier Badges
- **ENTRY (up to €50):** 1px border `#3F4248`, text `#9CA3AF`.
- **CORE (up to €90):** 1px border `#282A2E`, text `#EDE8DF`, background `#1A1D20`.
- **SIGNATURE (up to €140):** 1px border `#C28B38`, text `#E5A93C`, subtle copper background tint.
- **PREMIUM (€140+):** Solid brushed metallic finish border `#E5A93C`, text `#F9F8F6`, background `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(229,169,60,0.15) 0%, rgba(20,22,24,1) 100%)`.

### Inputs & Selectors
- Dark slate fill `#0C0D0E`, 1px border `#282A2E`, typography `body-md` in `#F9F8F6`.
- Focus state activates a 1px border `#D97706` and subtle inner ambient amber glow. Placeholders set in `#9CA3AF` at 60% opacity.
