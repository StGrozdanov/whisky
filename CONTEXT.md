# Whisky Finder

A boutique whisky shop for the Bulgarian market: a short selected list, shop-written video reviews, and a personalised Finder.

**Whisky Finder**:
The shop. Public site: whiskyfinder.bg. Contact: 0876473165, info@whiskyfinder.bg.
_Avoid_: whisky (as the shop’s name), boutique (as the name)

## Catalogue

**Whisky**:
The liquid people talk about — a named expression such as GlenAllachie 12. Listing one requires its Distillery, manually uploaded and ordered photos, Origin, Country, ABV, an Age statement choice, and at least one SKU. The first photo is the Catalogue image. It is publicly visible only after the Shopkeeper publishes it; a draft is unavailable by URL, Finder, Search, and Home. The Finder, House pick, House score, House video, and Awards attach here.
_Avoid_: SKU, bottle, product, item (when meaning the liquid)

**Distillery**:
The producer named for a Whisky, shown on its Whisky page and searchable with its name, Country, and Region.
_Avoid_: brand, bottler

**SKU**:
A sellable size of one Whisky, such as a 700ml bottle. The cart sells SKUs. The Shopkeeper changes each SKU’s quantity manually. Each SKU has a quantity and an Availability.
_Avoid_: Whisky, product (when meaning the liquid)

**Primary SKU**:
The full-bottle SKU the Shopkeeper selects to set a Whisky’s Catalogue price and Price tier. Other sizes remain selectable on the Whisky page.
_Avoid_: cheapest SKU, sample

**Pack**:
A SKU that contains several Whiskies, such as a gift set or a 50ml discovery pack. It has its own quantity, independent of the quantities of its component SKUs.
_Avoid_: Whisky

**Price tier**:
One of four price bands used by the Finder: ENTRY (up to €50), CORE (over €50 and up to €90), SIGNATURE (over €90 and up to €140), or PREMIUM (over €140). A Whisky’s tier comes from its primary full-bottle SKU at regular price; samples, Club price, and Promotion do not move it.
_Avoid_: category, quality level

**Gift**:
A Whisky or Pack prepared for another person, optionally with Shopkeeper-priced gift wrapping and a personal message, and deliverable directly to the recipient using their name, phone number, and Delivery destination. The Buyer remains the payer and receives the Order confirmation. It is not a gift card or voucher.
_Avoid_: voucher, gift card

**Promotion**:
A scheduled reduction of a SKU’s regular price with a start and end. The Customer sees the regular, discounted, and legally required prior price. The Shopkeeper keeps the prior-price record, but there is no price-history page. It does not use promotional codes, quantity discounts, or bundle discounts. A Promotion appears on Home only when it is both active and selected by the Shopkeeper.
_Avoid_: coupon, promo code, Club price

**Award**:
A named recognition attached to a Whisky, with its awarding organisation, year, and award category.
_Avoid_: House pick, House score

**Origin**:
The family a Whisky belongs to: Irish, Scotch, Bourbon, or Japanese. Filtered on the catalogue, not in the Finder.
_Avoid_: category, type, region (when meaning these four)

**Country**:
The country where a Whisky comes from, shown on its Whisky page.
_Avoid_: Origin, Region

**Region**:
An optional area within a Whisky’s Country, such as Speyside, Islay, or Kentucky.
_Avoid_: Origin, Country

**Age statement**:
The declared maturation years of a Whisky, or no declared age (NAS). The Catalogue can include either or both.
_Avoid_: maturity, release year

**Experience level**:
The Shopkeeper’s optional indication that a Whisky is for beginners or advanced drinkers. It filters the Catalogue but never affects Finder ranking.
_Avoid_: quality level, Price tier

**House score**:
An optional Shopkeeper score for a Whisky from 1.0 to 10.0, with at most one decimal place.
_Avoid_: Tasting score, review

**Displayed score**:
The House score when present, otherwise the average score from public Tastings. A Whisky with neither has no Displayed score and does not appear in score-filtered Catalogue results.
_Avoid_: Finder score, match percentage

**House video**:
An optional YouTube URL embedded on a Whisky page, in which the shop reviews that Whisky. The Shopkeeper does not upload or host video.
_Avoid_: Tasting, Customer review

**Catalogue**:
A list of Whiskies (not SKUs), ordered alphabetically by Whisky name with no Customer-controlled sorting and filterable only by Origin, Price tier, Age statement, Displayed score, and Experience level. The price shown is the In-stock SKU **Buy** would use; if none, an Ask-us SKU.
_Avoid_: product grid, SKU list, shop (when meaning this list)

**New Whisky**:
A Whisky the Shopkeeper manually selects for the Home new-Whiskies section. It has no automatic expiry.
_Avoid_: new release date

**Award page**:
The public list of Whiskies that have Awards. A Whisky may have more than one Award.
_Avoid_: House pick, House score

**Gift page**:
The public list of Shopkeeper-selected Whiskies and Packs suitable for gifting. Gift wrapping and a personal message are chosen at checkout.
_Avoid_: gift card, voucher

**About page**:
The public Whisky Finder story, its philosophy, contact details, and House editorial and video content.
_Avoid_: Contact request, House pick

**Main navigation**:
The public links to Catalogue, Gifts, Promotions, Award page, Club, and Finder. It does not include the About page.
_Avoid_: Useful links, Policies

**Useful links**:
Footer links repeating the Main navigation destinations, rather than a separately managed list of external resources.
_Avoid_: social links, Policies

**Policy pages**:
The public Terms, Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, Delivery & Payment, Withdrawal & Returns, and Age Policy pages linked from the footer.
_Avoid_: Useful links, Contact request

## Tasting

**Tasting**:
A Member’s public written contribution about a Whisky, with required text and an optional 1–10 score. The Shopkeeper approves, rejects with a reason, or hides it after publication; a rejected Tasting remains visible to its author with a reason and may be edited and resubmitted. Only approved Tastings are public. They show the author’s full first and last name, but never their email, Orders, or Favorites. A paid Order for the Whisky earns a verified-purchase mark, and only that Member’s own Tasting text and scores personalise their Finder. There is no Customer reporting flow.
_Avoid_: House score, House video, review

**Sweetness**:
A Finder choice: сладко, балансирано, or сухо и пикантно.

**Smoke**:
A Finder choice: без, средно, or силно.

**Intensity**:
A Finder choice: меко, балансирано, or смело и плътно.

**Fruit profile**:
A Finder choice: тъмни плодове (стафиди, смокини, синя слива), светли плодове (зелена ябълка, круша, цитрус), or баланс между двете.

**Finder**:
A five-step flow for Smoke, Intensity, Sweetness, Fruit profile, and an optional Price tier. On every run, AI searches approved live public sources without a cache and is the source of truth for relevance, including whether an Ask-us Whisky ranks above an In-stock one. The application limits results to Catalogue Whiskies, the correct Price tier, up to three per tier, and the correct Buy or Ask-us action. It gives a short reason without a score, percentage, or visible source evidence. Current answers take priority over personal history. If AI fails, the Customer sees an honest error and may retry; no invented or fallback results are shown.
_Avoid_: search, recommendations outside the Catalogue, displayed percentage

**Search**:
A name-only lookup across Whisky, Distillery, Country, and Region. A Country or Region result takes the Customer to the Catalogue with that filter applied. No match shows an honest empty state without AI suggestions or fallback Catalogue cards.
_Avoid_: Finder, full-text search

**Taste profile**:
A Member’s saved Finder answers and Finder history. Their own Tastings also personalise later Finder results. A guest’s history lasts only for the browser session and moves to an Account only with the Customer’s choice; Member history remains until the Member deletes it or the Account.
_Avoid_: Tasting, House tasting, public profile

**House pick**:
The one published Whisky the Shopkeeper manually features on Home, with a short story and optional period label. Editorial, not an award. Replaced when the shop has a new story; the calendar does not rotate it automatically.
_Avoid_: whisky of the month (as a contest), award, trophy, winner

**Home**:
The first page, containing the House pick, Club teaser, live Promotions, new Whiskies, Discovery Packs, and Finder call to action. The Shopkeeper manually selects entries for its rails. A data-driven section with no entries is omitted; Whisky cards are never invented for display.
_Avoid_: Catalogue, landing mockup

## Commerce

**Customer**:
An adult in Bulgaria buying for themselves, usually a curious beginner. They pass a one-time 18+ splash, then may browse. They may check out as a guest or as a Member.
_Avoid_: user, client, buyer

**Account**:
Optional verified email-and-password login for a Customer, with password recovery; checkout does not require it. Registration requires first and last name. It contains personal details, saved Delivery addresses, Orders, Favorites, Taste profile, Finder history, Tastings, and Club membership. It has no public profile page. Deletion logs the Customer out, removes personal details, addresses, Favorites, Taste profile, and Finder history, anonymises public Tastings, and retains Orders for the required accounting period.
_Avoid_: profile, user

**Member**:
A Customer who has an Account.
_Avoid_: subscriber, user

**Favorite**:
A Whisky saved by a Member to their Account. Guests cannot save Favorites and do not see Favorite actions.
_Avoid_: wishlist, saved SKU

**Club membership**:
A €60 non-physical membership purchase, payable only by Stripe card, that gives a Member Club benefits for 12 months from confirmed payment. It never renews automatically; a manual renewal extends an active membership by 12 months, while expiry does not remove the Account, Orders, Favorites, or Tastings.
_Avoid_: subscription, Account, Member

**Club price**:
The automatic 5% reduction from a SKU’s regular price for an active Club membership. It does not stack with a promotional price; the lower price wins.
_Avoid_: Member price, coupon, discount code

**Club bottle**:
One available SKU the Shopkeeper manually selects and ships free of charge to the Delivery address from the €60 Club membership checkout after payment is confirmed. It is not chosen by the Member, is not added to the membership checkout cart, and has no cash value.
_Avoid_: voucher, Points reward, subscription box

**Club offer**:
A Shopkeeper-selected SKU offer with a Club price, quantity, start, and end, visible only to active Club memberships. It disappears when it expires or quantity reaches zero. Early-access Whiskies may become public after their Club-only period.
_Avoid_: public promotion, Member price

**Newsletter subscription**:
A Customer’s separate marketing consent, created immediately from the submitted email without email confirmation. The Shopkeeper retains the submitted email, consent timestamp, and policy version. Every message offers unsubscribe; an unsubscribed address remains only on the suppression list.
_Avoid_: Account, Club membership, Member

**Cookie consent**:
No consent banner is shown while the site uses only strictly necessary cookies. Any later non-essential analytics or marketing cookie requires consent before use.
_Avoid_: Newsletter subscription, Account

**Contact request**:
A message with name, email, optional phone, subject, and text, sent without requiring an Account. The Shopkeeper sees it in back office. It does not send an automatic copy or confirmation to the Customer and has no Customer-visible workflow or status.
_Avoid_: Ask us, Waitlist

**Order**:
A confirmed purchase of In-stock SKUs, paid by card now or by cash on delivery. It retains the purchased Whisky name, SKU, and price if that Whisky is later unpublished. There is no Order state machine or courier tracking in the first version. A guest Order is not attached to an existing Account without authentication; after creating an Account, the Customer may link it through verified email.
_Avoid_: cart, transaction, payment

**Payment method**:
Stripe card payment or cash on delivery. There is no bank transfer, PayPal, Apple Pay, or Google Pay in the first version.
_Avoid_: Order, Delivery

**Invoice**:
A fiscal receipt or, on request, an invoice for an Order. The Customer can provide company billing details during checkout.
_Avoid_: Order confirmation, payment receipt

**Refund**:
A full or partial repayment against an Order. For a refused or uncollected cash-on-delivery Order, quantity and any Club bottle entitlement return only after the Shopkeeper physically receives the shipment.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Promotion

**Order confirmation**:
The durable page and email sent for an Order, containing its number, SKUs, applied prices, Delivery, total, payment method, and withdrawal information.
_Avoid_: Contact request, payment receipt

**Withdrawal request**:
A Customer’s notice that they want to exercise a legal right of withdrawal. It is made through the published phone, Contact request, or email channel and is manually processed by the Shopkeeper; it is not an Order cancellation or a dedicated self-service form.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, refund request

**Cart**:
The In-stock SKUs a Customer intends to buy. A guest Cart lives in their browser, a Member Cart lives in their Account, and the two merge on login by adding duplicate quantities up to Availability and telling the Customer when quantity is limited; price and Availability are checked again at checkout.
_Avoid_: Order

**Reservation**:
A 20-minute hold on SKU quantity while a Customer completes card payment. Confirmed payment turns it into an Order; failure or expiry releases it. After a failed card payment, the Customer may retry or choose cash on delivery. A cash-on-delivery Order commits quantity immediately without a Reservation.
_Avoid_: Order, Waitlist, back-order

**Delivery**:
Speedy courier shipment of an Order to an address or Speedy office in Bulgaria. After the Customer selects the destination, checkout shows the applicable Speedy rate before Order confirmation. There is no shop pickup or ID check at handoff.
_Avoid_: collection, click and collect, store pickup

**Availability**:
Whether a SKU can be bought now. The Customer sees two paths only: **In stock** (**Buy**) or **Ask us** (**Waitlist**). **On request** (the shop is not holding it) and **Sold out** (quantity is zero; the SKU stays listed) are shop notes for why it is not In stock, not Customer-facing labels.
_Avoid_: status, stock (when meaning this split)

**Ask us**:
The only Customer action on a SKU that is not In stock: join the Waitlist.
_Avoid_: Buy, contact, back-order, enquire

**Waitlist**:
A Customer request to be told when a SKU becomes In stock. It is not a Shopkeeper contact request. Email is required; phone is optional. A Member’s Account email is used. An email has at most one active entry for a SKU. No payment until it is In stock. When the SKU becomes In stock, one email goes to everyone on that Waitlist and those entries are cleared.
_Avoid_: back-order, reservation, ask-to-source

**Shopkeeper**:
The one person who signs into the protected back office with email and password, separately from a Customer Account. They manually enter and edit all Catalogue data, list, publish, and unpublish Whiskies; set Home rails, House tasting, House pick, quantity, Promotion, Club offers, and Club bottles; pack Orders; view Order details; manage Contact requests and Waitlists; and moderate Tastings. No other staff logins, dashboard analytics, exports, bulk operations, or general audit log exist in the first version.
_Avoid_: admin, user, staff, warehouse
